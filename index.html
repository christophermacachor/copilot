The documentary explains that cold and warm sensors are separate, and that in this "unnatural scenario," the brain gets confused: heat signals cancel out cold signals, leaving only pain sensors active.

"It's feeling cold, and it's also feeling warmth. And somehow, it's interpreting these two signals as pain." — Theanne Griffith, NOVA
